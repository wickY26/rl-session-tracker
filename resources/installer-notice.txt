PRIVACY
Everything the app records (your matches, sessions and the screenshots of the
scoreboard) stays on this PC, in %APPDATA%\rl-session-tracker\data. Screenshots
are read on this PC; nothing is uploaded.

The only network access: the app checks GitHub
(github.com/wickY26/rl-session-tracker/releases) for a newer version at start-up
and every 6 hours. This request contains no personal data; GitHub sees your IP
address as with any web request (GitHub's privacy statement applies:
https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
You can turn it off in Settings > App > "Check for updates automatically".
Updates are only downloaded and installed when you click to do so.
